Fix ClickHouse party aggregation lineage compatibility.

- Downstream party/event queries still referenced legacy source_start_line/source_end_line.

- party_agg now exposes compatibility aliases:
  source_start_line -> source_first_line
  source_end_line -> source_last_line

No database reset required.
